Medical Nurse

Full Time · On-site

Application Deadline: April 30, 2025

Requirements

  • Education: Diploma in Nursing (BNMC Registration required)
  • Experience: Minimum 2 years
  • Experience in Manufacturing (FMCG), Garments
  • Age: 20–35 years
  • Prior experience in industrial setup preferred
  • Ability to handle emergency situations efficiently
  • Strong interpersonal skills

Responsibilities & Context

Vision Garments Ltd is looking for a qualified Nurse for an RMG factory in Rajashan, Savar.

  • Provide first aid and emergency care
  • Maintain employee health records and periodic checkups
  • Administer prescribed medications
  • Monitor workplace hygiene and safety compliance
  • Assist in occupational health awareness programs
  • Coordinate hospital transfers when required
  • Maintain medical supply inventory

Skills & Expertise

Nursing, Patient Care, Primary Health Care

Compensation & Benefits

  • Overtime allowance & mobile bill
  • Yearly salary review
  • Festival bonus (2)
  • Leave encashment
  • Flexible leave policy
  • Other benefits as per company policy

Workplace: Office

Gender: Only Female

Location: Savar, Dhaka
