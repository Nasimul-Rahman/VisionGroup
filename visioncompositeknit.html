VISION COMPOSITE KNIT LTD-2007

Our buyers love us for working in harmony with them in developing styles of latest craze and our core values of timely delivery without compromising quality of high fashion knit shirts for ladies, men & kids. We make knit shirts from 1×1 Rib, 2×2 Rib, single jersey, 100% cotton, 100% polyester, interlock, CVC, TC, pique, lycra fabric from local and regional sources. Backed by our own sourcing knitting, dyeing and finishing facility and with our in-house button, embroidery, wash & garments printing from sister concerns, we endeavor to accommodate the newest fashion styles for all knit products. Factory has capacity to produce annually 3.4 million pcs knitwear and annually 4.6 million pcs fashionable casual shirts, blouses, dresses, baby set, ramper set and nightwear. The factory has received certification from a number of our buyers who are satisfied with our work practices. The 10,400 square meter factory is situated at Purbo Rajashan, Savar, and Just 23 Km away from the main airport of Dhaka and employs over 2,400 people.
