VISION ACCESSORIES LTD-2009

Vision Accessories Ltd annual turnover is USD 2.5Million and producing 0.3 million GG button in a year.The factory produces polyester buttons from 14 to 16 lines in lead free, phthalate free, cadmium free, laser engraved fancy buttons. all chemical are imported and all machines are branded Italy origin.

Since 2009 Vision Accessories has been producing:

  • Pearl Button
  • Horn Button
  • Chalk Button
  • Fancy Button
  • Logo Button

It has received the honor of nomination from leading brands H&M and C&A tosupply polyester buttons to their nominated RMG factories in Bangladesh. The 2500 square meter factory is situated at North rajashon, Savar just 23Km away from the main airport of Dhaka and employees over 150 people.
